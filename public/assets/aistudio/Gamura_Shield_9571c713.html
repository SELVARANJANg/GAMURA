The scams that fool people everywhere.

🏦 Fake bank or KYC alert“Your account is blocked, update KYC now.” Real banks do not ask you to confirm details through a link in a message. Open your bank’s official app instead.
🔢 OTP and PIN requestsNobody legitimate needs your OTP, PIN, CVV or password. Not support staff, not delivery agents, not “the bank”.
🎁 Prizes and lotteriesYou cannot win a contest you never entered. Asking for a fee to release a prize is the scam.
📦 Parcel and toll feesSmall “redelivery fee” links steal card details. Check the courier’s official site directly.
💼 Easy job offersPay for simple tasks, then a request to deposit money first. Genuine employers never charge you to start work.
❤️ Romance and investmentA new online friend pushing crypto or “guaranteed returns” is a classic pig-butchering scam.
👮 Fake authority callsPolice, tax or customs officers do not demand payment by gift card or crypto, or threaten instant arrest by phone.
