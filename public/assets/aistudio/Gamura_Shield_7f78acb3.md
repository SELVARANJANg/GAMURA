# Learn

The scams that fool people everywhere.

**🏦 Fake bank or KYC alert**“Your account is blocked, update KYC now.” Real banks do not ask you to confirm details through a link in a message. Open your bank’s official app instead.

**🔢 OTP and PIN requests**Nobody legitimate needs your OTP, PIN, CVV or password. Not support staff, not delivery agents, not “the bank”.

**🎁 Prizes and lotteries**You cannot win a contest you never entered. Asking for a fee to release a prize is the scam.

**📦 Parcel and toll fees**Small “redelivery fee” links steal card details. Check the courier’s official site directly.

**💼 Easy job offers**Pay for simple tasks, then a request to deposit money first. Genuine employers never charge you to start work.

**❤️ Romance and investment**A new online friend pushing crypto or “guaranteed returns” is a classic pig-butchering scam.

**👮 Fake authority calls**Police, tax or customs officers do not demand payment by gift card or crypto, or threaten instant arrest by phone.
