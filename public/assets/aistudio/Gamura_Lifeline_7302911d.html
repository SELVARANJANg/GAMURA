First Aid

Quick steps. Call emergency services first.

❤️ CPR (adult)
Start beat
  1. Check the scene is safe, shout for help, call emergency services.
  2. Place the heel of your hand on the centre of the chest, other hand on top.
  3. Push hard and fast, about 5–6 cm deep, 100–120 times per minute (follow the beat).
  4. Let the chest rise fully between pushes.
  5. Use an AED as soon as one arrives. Continue until help takes over.
🫁 Choking (adult)
  1. Ask “Are you choking?” If they cannot cough, speak or breathe, act.
  2. Give up to 5 firm back blows between the shoulder blades.
  3. Then up to 5 abdominal thrusts (inward and upward above the navel).
  4. Alternate until the object clears or they collapse. Then call emergency and start CPR.
🩸 Severe bleeding
  1. Call emergency services.
  2. Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth. Do not remove it if soaked; add more on top.
  3. Keep pressure on and raise the injured limb if possible.
  4. Keep the person lying down and warm until help arrives.
🔥 Burns
  1. Cool the burn under cool running water for 20 minutes.
  2. Remove rings or tight items before swelling starts, unless stuck to skin.
  3. Cover loosely with clean cling film or a non-fluffy cloth.
  4. No ice, butter or creams. Seek urgent care for large, deep or facial burns.
🧠 Stroke signs (FAST)
  1. Face drooping on one side.
  2. Arm weakness, cannot raise both.
  3. Speech slurred or confused.
  4. Time: call emergency immediately and note when symptoms began.
