# Manual

Everything you need to make a video in GAMURA VEO.

1 · Quick start

Tap a tool on the home screen, or Start a new video. Add videos or images, tap a clip on the timeline to select it, edit it with the tabs at the bottom, then open Export and tap Render & download.

2 · Clips & merge

Clips play in timeline order, so adding several videos merges them. Use Move to reorder, Split to cut at the playhead, Duplicate to repeat, Delete to remove and Undo to step back.

3 · Trim, crop, rotate

Edit tab: Trim start and end cut the clip, Speed changes pace, Crop zoom with Pan X/Y crops the frame, Rotate turns it, and Fit + blur keeps the whole video on a blurred background.

4 · Photo to video & motion

Images become videos automatically. Set Duration in Edit and pick a move in Motion: zoom, pan, drift, shake or Beat pulse (set the BPM of your song). Fade or Flash sets the transition in.

5 · Looks, text & stickers

Looks: pick an Aura look, then adjust brightness, contrast, saturation, blur and vignette. Text: type a caption, choose position, size and colour, and add an emoji sticker.

6 · Logo & app logo

Logo tab: upload a watermark, set size, opacity and corner. Tap the G badge on the home screen (or Set app logo) to use your own GAMURA VEO logo, and Use app logo as watermark to stamp it on videos.

7 · Song & voiceover

Audio tab: add a song, set its volume, start point and fade-out. Move the playhead where you want to speak, press Record, talk, then press Stop. Use headphones to avoid echo.

8 · Magic tools

Auto-edit adds motion, fades and a cinematic look in one tap. Title card makes a gradient intro. Save snapshot downloads the current frame. Mute all removes original audio.

9 · Export

Choose 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, then HD or Full HD. Rendering runs in real time, so keep the tab open until it finishes. The file saves as MP4 or WebM.

10 · Shortcuts & fixes

Desktop: Space play/pause, ←/→ jump 1 s, S split, Delete remove clip. If the mic fails, allow microphone access. For best results use Chrome or Edge.
